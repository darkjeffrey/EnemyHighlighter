# Changelog

## 1.0.1
- Updated for Bannerlord v1.5.7.
- The launcher no longer warns that the dependencies (Native, Harmony, ButterLib, UIExtenderEx, MCM) don't match: each one now states the version it needs.

## 1.0.0
- Hold a key (Left Alt by default) to outline every enemy near you, or switch to toggle mode.
- Works in field battles, sieges, hideouts, arena fights, tournaments and fights in settlements.
- Options for the key, outline colour (including colour-blind friendly ones), distance, outline limit, seeing outlines through walls, fleeing enemies and horses, and which kinds of scene use it.
